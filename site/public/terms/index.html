1. The service

Pescada is an online portal where the Customer exchanges documents with its own clients, tracks each document from sending to confirmation, keeps an activity log, receives reminders for recurring deadlines, and can have bank statements and invoices read automatically and matched.

2. Accounts and users

  • The Customer decides who gets access: its own staff and the people it invites at each of its clients. It is responsible for their use of the service and for removing access when someone leaves.

3. Customer data

Documents and data uploaded to Pescada belong to the Customer or its clients. We use them only to provide the service, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement, which forms part of these terms.

4. Acceptable use

The Customer will not upload unlawful content or malware, try to access other customers’ data, test the service’s security without our written permission, or resell the service without agreement.

5. Automated reading

Data extracted from documents and suggested matches between payments and invoices are aids to the Customer’s work. Some are produced by AI and can contain errors. The Customer reviews them before relying on them for accounting entries, filings or advice.

6. The activity log

The log records events that happen in Pescada: uploads, deliveries, notifications reported by the mail provider, openings, downloads, confirmations and notes, with their time and author. It does not record what happens outside Pescada, for example whether someone read an email.
