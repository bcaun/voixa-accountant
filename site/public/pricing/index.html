How the free trial works

  1. Sign up with your firm’s name and email; open the link we send and you are in.
  2. For 30 days you have everything in Pro, without a card. During the trial a firm can have up to 10 clients and send up to 20 invitations a day.
  3. Subscribe to Essential or Pro whenever you are ready. If you do not, everything is kept: you can still read and export it, and your clients can still send documents.
