Product

Built for how accounting firms actually work, in five countries.

One firm, every client

A workspace for each client

Each company has its own space and can invite its own staff. Clients never see each other; your team sees them all.

Monthly · quarterly · yearly

Deadlines that open themselves

Templates for each country, from Modelo 303 to F24, CA3, UStVA and the Portuguese VAT return: requests appear ahead of time, with reminders.

FatturaPA · XRechnung · Factur-X · bank files

Statements and e-invoices read for you

E-invoices and bank files are read exactly, or fetched from the bank through open banking; on the Pro plan, PDFs and scans are read by AI too. Counterparty, tax ID, amount and invoice numbers come out of every payment.

Suggested, then confirmed

Payments matched to invoices

Each payment is matched to the invoices the client uploaded, by number, amount, NIF and date. Your team confirms or rejects every match.

SHA-256 · CSV · printable report

An evidence log you can hand over

Every action sits in an append-only, hash-chained log. Export it as CSV, or print a tracking report for one document.

Full history

Versions, never overwrites

Replacing a file keeps the earlier version and the reason, and asks the other side to open and confirm again.
