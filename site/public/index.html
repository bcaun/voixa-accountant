The problem

Three sentences every accountant has heard.

I never got it.

Pescada shows when the file arrived, when the notification reached their inbox and when they opened it. Receipt can only be confirmed after opening.

You never sent it.

Every upload is fingerprinted and time-stamped in a log that cannot be edited afterwards. Your client sees the same record you do.

It went to spam.

The receiving mail server reports back: delivered, bounced or marked as spam. Anything left unopened gets an automatic reminder.

How it works

From upload to confirmed, in four recorded steps.

  1. Sent

    The client drops files into their private workspace, or you send a report with its deadline.

  2. Notified

    The other side gets an email. Delivery, bounces and spam reports are written to the log.

  3. Opened

    Every opening and download is recorded, version by version, with the person and the time.

  4. Confirmed

    Receipt or approval takes one click, and is only possible once the file has been opened.

One firm, every client

Monthly · quarterly · yearly

Deadlines that open themselves
